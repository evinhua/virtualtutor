VirtualTutor

On-device voice tutoring. Speak naturally — pause to get a reply.

Idle

Press Start, then say something to begin.

Configuration

Voices

Kokoro voice for each spoken language. Applies to the tutor’s next sentence.

Duplex mode

Keep listening while the tutor speaks, so you can interrupt it. Use headphones — on speakers the tutor hears itself and cuts its own reply short. Off means half duplex: the mic is muted while it speaks.
